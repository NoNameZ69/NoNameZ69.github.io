Effective: 24 October 2026 · Android package: org.nocturne.neontower

This policy explains how Neon Tower, developed by SPACE AURORA, handles information. The game does not require a player account. Game progress is saved on your device. Advertising and optional purchases use Google’s services as described below.

Support: spaceaurora.apps@gmail.com. A permanent No Ads upgrade is available through Google Play. The game does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera or precise location.

1. Data stored on your device

Neon Tower stores your scores, completed levels, best completion times, earned stars, settings and equipped or unlocked block skins locally. It also stores an age-group choice, advertising preferences, the number of completed optional Wood-unlock ads and signed purchase entitlement records used to restore Gold and the No Ads upgrade. We do not ask for your date of birth, name or email to play.

The age-group choice is saved locally to select appropriate advertising restrictions. The game sends age-treatment flags to Google’s advertising services rather than your date of birth. There is no developer-operated account system, public leaderboard or separate gameplay analytics service in this release. Optional cloud saves use Google Play Games Saved Games.

Google Play Games cloud saves

You can enable cloud saving from Settings → Connect Play Games. Gameplay and purchases do not require cloud saving. When enabled, the game sends completed-level history, scores, best times, earned-star claims, unlocked cosmetic progress and game settings to Google Play Games Saved Games. It reads your Play Games player identifier to select the correct private save; a hash is stored locally to separate accounts. The game does not request your email address, contacts or friends list.

When saves conflict, the game preserves the furthest campaign progress and best recorded times and avoids awarding the same stars twice. Paid ownership is verified separately through Google Play Billing and is not granted by a cloud record. Synchronization requires a working connection; offline changes remain local until a successful sync. Play Games also processes gamer identity, SDK analytics and diagnostics as described in Google’s Play Games data disclosures. Data is encrypted in transit.

Delete Neon Tower cloud progress

  1. Close Neon Tower. Open your Google Play Games profile with the account used for this game.
  2. Open Your data → Delete Play Games account and data. In the section for individual games, choose Neon Tower → Delete and follow Google’s confirmation.
  3. To remove the device copy too, use Android Settings → Apps → Neon Tower → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the game. Do this on other devices with this save too, so an old local copy is not uploaded again.

This removes the selected cloud save and, when app storage is cleared, local scores, campaign progress, medals, cosmetic progress and settings. We have no separate server copy. Google Play purchases are handled separately: removing progress does not refund a purchase or remove Google’s transaction records. Google controls retention of its service and transaction records under its policies. See Google’s deletion instructions, or contact support for help without sending passwords or payment details.

2. Advertising and privacy choices

The game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google’s User Messaging Platform. Optional rewarded ads can provide one revive in an endless run or progress toward unlocking the Wood skin. Occasional interstitial ads may appear at natural breaks between levels or after longer endless runs. Ads do not appear over active gameplay. The app requests non-personalized, general-audience ads.

Google’s advertising services may process an IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad and app interactions, technical performance or diagnostic information, and supported device or account-related identifiers for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. The game removes permission to collect the Android advertising ID and does not request Android AdServices advertising-ID, attribution or Topics permissions. Child and under-age restrictions further limit eligible processing and advertising. These restrictions do not mean that all technical network processing is eliminated.

Where required, Google’s consent interface is shown before advertising is initialized. You can revisit applicable consent options from Settings → Privacy options or the Shop’s Privacy options button. The one-time No Ads upgrade removes all advertising. With a locally verified No Ads entitlement, the app does not initialize advertising on startup or request new ads. Revive remains limited to once per endless run without an ad, and Wood is included. Privacy and consent choices are respected regardless of purchase status.

Read Google’s Privacy Policy, Google’s advertising information and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures. Data handled by Google is subject to its policies, regional controls and retention practices. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

3. Children and families

Neon Tower can be played by children and adults. A neutral age-group screen is used before enabling advertising. Users under 13 receive child-directed advertising treatment; other minor age bands receive under-age protections. The game requests a general-audience ad rating and non-personalized ads, and does not include third-party ad mediation networks.

We do not knowingly ask children for personal contact details, photos, audio recordings or precise location. Purchases use Google Play’s payment interface. Google Play’s purchase authorization and family settings apply. Parents can contact support with privacy questions without including a child’s personal details.

4. Google Play purchases

The optional Gold skin and permanent No Ads upgrade are separate one-time, non-consumable Google Play purchases. The price shown by Google Play is the applicable local price. Google Play processes payment and payment-account details; the game does not receive your card number or billing credentials.

The app receives product identifiers, purchase status, a purchase token and a signed purchase record to verify, acknowledge and restore the entitlement. A purchase is not granted while payment is pending. Verified purchase data is cached locally so ownership can remain available offline and is refreshed through Google Play. After reinstalling, ownership is checked automatically with the purchasing Google Play account. No separate Restore button is needed. Purchase and refund records held by Google are governed by Google’s policies and applicable financial retention requirements.

5. Retention, deletion and your choices

Local game data remains until it is removed through Android’s app-storage controls or the app is uninstalled. Clearing storage deletes local progress and settings. The game’s Android backup setting is disabled; optional Play Games Saved Games is separate from Android backup. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not automatically delete an existing Play Games cloud save. Clearing local data does not refund a purchase or delete Google Play’s transaction record; eligible purchases can be restored.

There is no Neon Tower player account to delete. To ask about information you have sent directly to support, email the support address. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or a child’s personal information. If a request concerns Google’s advertising or payment data, Google’s privacy and account controls may also be needed. Rights and available choices depend on your region.

6. This website and updates

This support page has no advertising, analytics script, sign-in or contact form. Its hosting provider may process ordinary connection and security logs when serving it. Emailing support sends your message and email address to the support mailbox. Links to Google open services governed by Google’s own policies.

We may update this policy when the game or its service providers change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
